## The inbox

Each notification shows what happened, the brief or person it's about, and when. Click one to open it. **Mark all as read** clears the badge.

Filter by **Type** (Reports submitted, Reports approved, Changes requested, Reports sent, Team joins, Drafts ready) and **Read status** (Unread only, or All).

## What lands here

| Event                        | You get it when                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Brief submitted for approval | Someone submits a brief you can approve.                               |
| Brief approved               | A reviewer approves your brief.                                        |
| Changes requested            | A reviewer sends your brief back.                                      |
| Brief sent                   | Your approved brief goes out to the client.                            |
| New team member              | Someone joins the workspace (owners and managers).                     |
| Email delivery failure       | An email you triggered couldn't be delivered. In-app only.             |
| Draft ready                  | An [automatic draft](/app/clients/#automatic-drafts) was made for you. |

You're never notified about your own action.

## Choosing what you get

**Notification settings →** on the inbox, or **Settings → Notifications**, has an **In-app** and an **Email** switch for each event. They're your own choices for this workspace. See [Notifications settings](/settings/notifications/) for the defaults and [Team notification emails](/emails/team-notifications/) for what each email looks like.
