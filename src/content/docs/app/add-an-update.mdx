## Adding an update

1. Click the pencil button, or choose **Add an update** for a client in [search](/app/search-and-help/).
2. Pick the client. **Adding to** shows which brief the note will go into; change the period there if needed.
3. Type what happened. **Enter** adds it, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. Or press the microphone and speak.

Each update you add is listed under the box so you can see where it went. Made a mistake? **Undo** appears in the confirmation for a few seconds.

## Where updates go

Updates go into the client's brief for the current period. If that brief doesn't exist yet, it's created as a draft with the first update, unless the client's schedule makes drafts ahead of time (see [automatic drafts](/app/clients/#automatic-drafts)).

Notes land in a **General** section at the end of the brief. In the editor, each General note offers **Move to** a section, or **Fine as is** to leave it there.

What a contributor sees of the rest of the brief depends on the client's **Team contribution mode**: just the section headings, the whole brief read-only, or full editing. See [Client settings](/app/clients/#client-settings).

## When updates can't be added

The panel tells you why, for example:

- The client's template has contributions turned off.
- The template has no regular cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly or quarterly), so there's no current period to add to.
- The client has no template and the workspace has no default template.

Fix these in [Templates](/settings/templates/).
