<aside class="sb-tldr">
  <p class="sb-tldr-label">TL;DR</p>
  <strong>Activity</strong> has three tabs: <strong>Workspace Activity</strong> (who did what),{' '}
  <strong>Email Delivery</strong> (whether each brief email was delivered, opened or bounced) and{' '}
  <strong>Advanced</strong> (the full audit log, kept for 7 years).
</aside>

## Workspace Activity

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/app/activity.webp"
  dark="/screenshots/app/activity-dark.webp"
  alt="The Workspace Activity tab: search, action and date filters, and a table of events (report approved, rejected, submitted, created, sent, schedule created) with when, who and the brief."
/>

A feed of what happened in the workspace: briefs created, submitted, approved, sent back and sent, schedules created and more. Filter by text, **Action** and date range, then **Apply**. Click a brief to open it.

## Email Delivery

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/app/activity-email.webp"
  dark="/screenshots/app/activity-email-dark.webp"
  alt="The Email Delivery tab: From and To dates, a Reset to 30 days button, and one row per sent brief with its client, recipients and delivered, opened, clicked and bounced counts."
/>

One row per sent brief with what happened to each email: **delivered**, **opened**, **clicked** or **bounced**. Pick a date range, or **Reset to 30 days**. Open a row to see each recipient's events in order.

Opens and clicks only appear when [engagement tracking](/settings/email-sending/#engagement-tracking) is on. Automatic opens by mail scanners are left out.

## Advanced

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/app/activity-advanced.webp"
  dark="/screenshots/app/activity-advanced-dark.webp"
  alt="The Advanced tab, Audit Logs: Action, Resource Type, Who, Start Date and End Date filters, and a table of events with a Details link on each."
/>

The full audit log of the workspace, retained for 7 years. Filter by **Action**, **Resource Type**, **Who** and date. **Details** shows the full record of an event.
