## The list

Each row shows the person, the clients they belong to with their role at each, and their **Delivery** status:

| Status            | Meaning                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Receiving**     | Gets briefs by email.                                                                                                            |
| **Not receiving** | Receive reports is off for every client.                                                                                         |
| **Bounced**       | Their address bounced or complained, so briefs stopped. See [Suppressed contacts](/settings/email-sending/#suppressed-contacts). |

The quick filters show **Everyone**, **Delivery problems** or **Not receiving**. You can also filter by status (active or archived), role and client.

Tick several rows to **Set role…**, archive or delete them together. Deleting is permanent; their send history is kept.

## Add a contact

Adding, editing and deleting contacts is for owners and managers, because contacts decide who receives briefs.

**Add contact** asks for the person's full name, email, optional phone, the client they belong to, their role there, and whether they **Receive reports**.

## Import from CSV

**Import CSV** bulk-imports contacts into one client. Choose the target client, then drop in a CSV file with these columns:

```text
full_name, email, role, phone, receive_reports
```

Rows with duplicate or invalid emails are listed so you can fix them before importing.

## A contact's page

| Card                | What it does                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Client**          | Each client this person belongs to, with their **Role** and a **Reports** switch. **Link to another client** adds them to a second client. **Unlink** removes them from one. |
| **Briefs received** | Every brief sent to this person.                                                                                                                                             |
| **Delivery**        | How many clients they receive briefs for, and the address used.                                                                                                              |

**Edit** changes the name, email or phone. Future briefs go to the new address; past delivery history stays with the contact. **More** archives, restores or deletes the contact.
