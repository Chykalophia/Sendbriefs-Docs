## Switching

Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to switch to another workspace you belong to. Everything you see (clients, briefs, team, settings) belongs to the current workspace.

## The Workspaces page

Lists every workspace you belong to with its plan, status, client count and creation date. Filter by plan (**Trial**, **Pro**, **Enterprise**) or status (**Active**, **Past Due**). **Settings →** opens that workspace's [settings](/settings/).

## Create a workspace

**Create Workspace** asks for a name (what your team will see) and a unique subdomain. You become its owner. Each workspace has its own plan and billing.
