## Roles

| Role        | Can                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Owner**   | Everything, including billing, workspace settings and deleting the workspace.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Manager** | Manage clients, contacts, templates and team members; approve, send and resend briefs.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Member**  | Write and edit any brief in the workspace, add updates and create clients. Can't add or edit contacts. By default a member can't submit, approve or send; turn those on per member with [brief permissions](#brief-permissions). A member can always withdraw a brief from review back to draft. |

Change someone's role with the picker on their row. **Remove** takes them out of the workspace.

## The list

Tabs show **Active**, **Suspended** or **All** members, and the chips filter by role. **Any activity** narrows to people with or without briefs in the last 90 days. **Seats used** counts against your [plan](/settings/plan-and-billing/).

## Invite or add someone

  src="/screenshots/app/team-invite.webp"

| Button            | What happens                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Invite member** | Enter an email and role. They get an [invitation email](/emails/team-notifications/) and join when they accept. Pending invitations are listed with **Resend** and **Revoke**. |
| **Add member**    | Enter a name, email and role to create the account now. If they don't have a SendBriefs account yet, they're emailed a link to set their password.                             |

If your plan has no free seats, you're offered **Upgrade plan**.

### Access

**Suspend access** ends their access to this workspace without deleting anything they wrote. Restore it from the same place.

### Brief permissions

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/app/team-member-permissions.webp"
  dark="/screenshots/app/team-member-permissions-dark.webp"
  alt="The Access card with a Suspend access button, and the Brief permissions card with three switches: Submit briefs for review, Approve their own briefs, Send their own briefs."
/>

These switches apply to members only; owners and managers can already do all three.

| Permission                   | Lets the member                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Submit briefs for review** | Submit any brief in the workspace for review.                      |
| **Approve their own briefs** | Approve briefs they created. The approval records who approved it. |
| **Send their own briefs**    | Send and resend briefs they created.                               |

Your [approval settings](/settings/approvals/) can still require someone else's approval.
