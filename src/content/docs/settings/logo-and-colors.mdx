## Workspace logo

Click or drag an image onto the upload area: PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 2 MB. **Use image URL instead** lets you paste a link.

## Colours

Pick a **colour theme**: Default, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Berry, Slate, Coral, Navy, Plum, Teal, Charcoal or Crimson. Or set **Custom colors** with your own **Primary** and **Secondary** hex values. The preview below shows the result. **Reset to default** goes back to the standard theme.

Clients with their own accent and primary colours, logo or brand mark use those instead. See [Client settings → Branding](/app/clients/#client-settings).

## Portal landing page

With a [custom domain](/settings/general/#custom-domain), your portal has a branded landing page. Set its **Headline**, **Subtitle**, **Call-to-action label** and background: **Brand color blend**, **Flat brand color** or **Your own background** image. Without the custom domain add-on, this card offers the upgrade.
