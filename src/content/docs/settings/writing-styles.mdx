## Presets

| Style                   | Writes                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Default**             | 1–3 short paragraphs of plain, warm-professional prose, with no jargon, IDs or headings. |
| **Action items**        | A short list of concrete next steps, each starting with a verb.                          |
| **Executive summary**   | 2–4 sentences: the headline outcome, the key metric, what happens next.                  |
| **Detailed highlights** | Grouped bullets under themes such as Progress, Metrics and Next.                         |

One style is always the default; it's pre-selected on every section. Pick another per section in the editor.

## Managing styles

- **New style**: give it a **Name** and **Instructions** describing the format and voice, for example "Friendly recap".
- **Default** (star): make it the default.
- **Edit** (pencil) or **Delete** (bin).
- **Reset preset**: put a preset back to its original name and instructions.

The AI's grounding and safety rules always apply. A style changes format and voice, never what the AI is allowed to say.
