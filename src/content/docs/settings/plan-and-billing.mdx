<aside class="sb-tldr">
  <p class="sb-tldr-label">TL;DR</p>
  <strong>Settings → Plan and billing</strong> shows your plan and usage, lets you redeem a code,
  add <strong>White-label</strong> or <strong>Custom domain</strong>, and opens the subscription
  portal for payment details and invoices. Only the owner can change billing.
</aside>

| Card                   | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Current Plan**       | Your plan and its status. **Upgrade Plan** compares plans.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Current Usage**      | Users, clients and briefs this month, against your plan's limits.                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Have a code?**       | Enter a code or paste a `/redeem` link. Codes apply a permanent entitlement to this workspace; you get a [confirmation email](/emails/billing-and-admin-emails/).                                                            |
| **Add-ons**            | **White-label** removes the "Sent via SendBriefs" line from every email. **Custom domain** serves brief links on your own domain; see [General → Custom domain](/settings/general/#custom-domain). **Add to plan** adds one. |
| **Billing & Invoices** | **View Available Plans**, and **Subscription Portal** to update your payment method, download invoices and see billing history.                                                                                              |

Current prices are on the plans page and at [sendbriefs.com/pricing](https://sendbriefs.com/pricing).
