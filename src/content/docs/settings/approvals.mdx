## Workspace approval policy

**Require approval before sending any brief**: when on, briefs go from draft to pending approval to approved before they can be sent, and members don't see a **Send** button.

## Approval flows

**New Approval Flow** adds a rule:

| Field                   | Options                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Scope**               | **Workspace Default**, **Role-Based** (Workspace Owner, Manager or Member), **Client-Specific** or **User-Specific**.                |
| **Approval Required**   | **Off** (no approval), **On** (required, can be overridden by a more specific rule) or **Enforced** (required, can't be overridden). |
| **Can Approve Reports** | People at this level can approve.                                                                                                    |
| **Can Send Reports**    | People at this level can send approved briefs.                                                                                       |

**Current Settings** lists every rule; the bin deletes one.

## Which rule wins

Rules are checked in this order, and the first that applies wins:

**Enforced workspace** → **User** → **Client** → **Role** → **Workspace default**

So an enforced workspace rule beats everything; otherwise a rule for a specific person beats one for their client, which beats one for their role.

## Other places that affect approval

- A [template](/settings/templates/#settings-strip) can require approval for briefs made from it.
- A member's [brief permissions](/app/team/#brief-permissions) can let them approve or send their own briefs.

If your plan doesn't include approval workflows, the template switch says so and offers an upgrade.
