## Require two-factor authentication

**Require MFA for all members** makes every member set up two-factor authentication before they can use the workspace. When you turn it on, members get a 7-day grace period to enrol. **MFA adoption** shows how many members already have it.

## Session timeout

**Idle timeout** signs people out after a period of inactivity: **15 minutes**, **1 hour**, **4 hours**, **8 hours**, **24 hours** or **Never**. Someone in several workspaces is held to the strictest timeout among them.

## IP allowlist

Restrict the app to specific networks. Requests from other addresses are blocked.

1. Enter a range in CIDR form, such as `203.0.113.0/24`, or a single address, such as `203.0.113.7`. Add an optional label like "Office VPN". Click **Add**. **Add my IP** adds the address you're on now.
2. Turn on the allowlist.

The page shows **Your IP** and whether it's **covered**. If it isn't, you're warned before you lock yourself out.

**Locked out?** **Email recovery link** sends you a one-time link that turns the allowlist off. See [IP allowlist recovery email](/emails/billing-and-admin-emails/).
