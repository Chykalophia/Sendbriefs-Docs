  <p class="sb-tldr-label">TL;DR</p>
  Four one-off emails: a <strong>subscription confirmation</strong> when a paid plan or trial
  starts, a <strong>redemption code</strong> when SendBriefs gives you one, a short{' '}
  <strong>sender check</strong> when you test your own sending account, and a{' '}
  <strong>recovery link</strong> for an owner locked out by the IP allowlist.
</aside>

## Subscription confirmed

**Who gets it:** the workspace's billing email address.
**When:** a paid plan or a trial starts.
**Subject:** Your SendBriefs subscription is confirmed

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/emails/subscription-confirmed.webp"
  alt="Email titled Subscription confirmed: Your SendBriefs Business plan is active and renews automatically on a date for a monthly price, and each month after that. You can cancel anytime from your billing settings. Manage subscription button."
  width={640}
/>

It states the plan, the renewal date and the price, and that the plan renews until you cancel. During a trial it says when the trial turns into a paid subscription. **Manage subscription** opens [Plan and billing](/settings/plan-and-billing/).

Invoices and receipts come from Stripe, not from this email. See [Invoices and receipts](/billing/invoices/).

## Redemption code

**Who gets it:** the person SendBriefs issued a code to.
**When:** SendBriefs staff send you a code, for example a plan or an add-on as part of an offer.
**Subject:** Your SendBriefs redemption code

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/emails/redemption-code.webp"
  alt="Email titled You've got a SendBriefs code: Unlocks the Business plan for your workspace. The code is shown in a grey box, with a black Redeem now button and the link to paste."
  width={640}
/>

**Redeem now** applies the code to the workspace you're signed in to. Make sure you're in the right workspace before you click.

## Sender verification

**Who gets it:** you, when you click **Verify** on a sending account in [Email sending](/settings/email-sending/).
**When:** you add your own sending account (your SMTP server or email provider) and test it.
**Subject:** SendBriefs sender verification

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/emails/sender-verification.webp"
  alt="A one-line email: This confirms your Harbor and Pine SMTP sender works. Your briefs can now send through it."
  width={640}
/>

The email is sent through the account you're testing, so receiving it proves the account works. If it doesn't arrive, the account shows an error instead.

## IP allowlist recovery

**Who gets it:** the workspace owner.
**When:** the owner asks for a recovery link because the [IP allowlist](/settings/security/#ip-allowlist) is blocking them.
**Subject:** Disable your SendBriefs IP allowlist

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/emails/ip-allowlist-recovery.webp"
  alt="Email: You requested a recovery link to disable the IP allowlist on your SendBriefs workspace. A link reads Disable the IP allowlist. This link can be used once and expires in 1 hour."
  width={640}
/>

The link turns the allowlist off. It works once and for one hour. If you didn't ask for it, ignore it and the allowlist stays on.
