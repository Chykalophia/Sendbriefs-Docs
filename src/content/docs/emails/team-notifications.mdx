## How they work

Every notification has two channels, and each person sets both:

- **In-app**: a row in the bell menu and on the [Notifications](/app/notifications/) page.
- **Email**: one of the emails on this page, sent to the address on your account.

The switches are per person and per workspace. If you belong to two workspaces, set them in each. Changes apply to the next event; nothing already sent is recalled.

Every email has a **Manage email notifications** link in the footer that opens your notification settings.

## Report ready for approval

**Who gets it:** everyone allowed to approve this brief, except the person who submitted it. That is anyone whose role can approve briefs, narrowed by the [approval rules](/settings/approvals/) for the brief's client.
**When:** a writer submits a brief for approval.
**Setting:** _Reports submitted for approval_. Email is on by default.

## Changes requested

**Who gets it:** the person who wrote the brief.
**When:** a reviewer sends the brief back instead of approving it.
**Setting:** _Changes requested on your reports_. Email is on by default.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/emails/changes-requested.webp"
  alt="Email titled Changes requested. Priya Raman has requested changes to October performance brief. A box shows the reviewer's feedback, then a Review comments list with a Must fix item quoting the sentence it is about and a Suggestion, then 'And 1 more on the brief' and an Edit Report button."
  width={640}
/>

The email carries the reviewer's note and up to the first few review comments, each labelled **Must fix**, **Suggestion** or **Note**. A comment pinned to a sentence quotes that sentence. If there are more comments than fit, the email says how many more are on the brief.

When the review has comments, the button opens the brief's feedback list. Without comments it opens the editor.

## Report approved

**Who gets it:** the person who wrote the brief, when someone else approved it.
**When:** a reviewer approves the brief.
**Setting:** _Your reports approved_. Email is on by default.

## Must-fix items resolved

**Who gets it:** the reviewer who asked for the fixes.
**When:** the writer resolves the last must-fix comment, so the brief can go out. The email says whether it is being sent now or is ready to send.
**Setting:** uses the _Your reports approved_ switch.

## Report sent

**Who gets it:** the person who wrote the brief, when someone else sent it.
**When:** the brief is emailed to the client.
**Setting:** _Your reports sent_. Email is **off** by default; the bell notification is on.

## New team member

**Who gets it:** workspace owners and managers.
**When:** someone accepts an invitation and joins the workspace.
**Setting:** _New team members_. Email is **off** by default.

## Draft ready

**Who gets it:** the client's brief owner.
**When:** SendBriefs creates the next brief for a client on its own, ahead of the deadline. This happens only for clients with **Automatic drafts** turned on in their [client settings](/app/clients/#client-settings).
**Setting:** _Drafts ready for you_. Email is **off** by default.

## Team invitation

**Who gets it:** the person you invite from [Team](/app/team/).
**When:** you send or resend an invitation. It isn't a notification, so there is no switch for it.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/emails/team-invitation.webp"
  alt="Email titled You've been invited to join Harbor and Pine Digital. Jordan Ellis has invited you to join their workspace on SendBriefs as Member. Accept Invitation button. This invitation expires in 7 days."
  width={640}
/>

The invitation names the role they will join with. The link works for 7 days; after that, resend it from the Team page.

## Notifications that never email

Two notifications only ever appear in the app:

- **Email delivery failures.** If an email you triggered can't be delivered by any of the workspace's sending accounts, you get a bell notification. Email can't report its own outage, so there is no email version. The note tells you whether to retry from the brief or to check with the recipient first, because a timed-out attempt may still have arrived.
- **Approved or sent by a trusted member.** When a member who has **Approve their own briefs** or **Send their own briefs** turned on (on their [Team](/app/team/) profile) uses it, the people who would normally approve get a bell notification, so a skipped review is still visible.
