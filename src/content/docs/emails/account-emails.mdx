All of these come from **SendBriefs**, not from your agency, and use the same plain black-and-white style. They never contain your password.

## Confirm your account

**When:** you sign up with an email address and password.
**What to do:** click **Verify Email Address**. You can't sign in until you do. The link works for 24 hours.
**Subject:** Confirm your SendBriefs account

Signing up with Google skips this email, because Google has already confirmed the address.

## Password reset

**When:** you click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page, or a workspace admin adds you to a workspace directly (the email is then your link to set a first password).
**What to do:** click **Set a new password** within 60 minutes. The link works once.
**Subject:** Your SendBriefs password reset request

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/emails/auth-reset-password.webp"
  alt="Email titled Reset your password: We received a request to reset the password for your SendBriefs account. This link expires in 60 minutes. Black Set a new password button, a copyable link, and a note that you can ignore the email if you didn't request it."

/>

If you didn't ask for it, ignore it. Your password stays the same until someone uses the link.

## Security alerts

SendBriefs emails you when a way of signing in to your account is added or removed. If you made the change, there's nothing to do. If you didn't, open **Review Security Settings** and reset your password.

| Alert                                      | Subject                                             |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Two-factor added                           | Two-factor authentication added to your account     |
| Two-factor removed                         | Two-factor authentication removed from your account |
| Sign-in method linked (for example Google) | A new sign-in method was linked to your account     |
| Sign-in method removed                     | A sign-in method was removed from your account      |

## Emails you're unlikely to see

These templates exist, but the app only sends them in support-assisted cases:

- **Confirm your new email address** (_Confirm your new email address_). You can't change your email from your profile; support changes it for you, and both addresses must confirm.
- **Sign-in link** (_Your SendBriefs sign-in link_). The sign-in page uses a password, Google or a passkey, not emailed links.
- **Confirm your identity** (_Confirm your identity_). A one-time code for a password change. Changing your password from your profile asks for your current password instead.
- **Invited to SendBriefs** (_You've been invited to SendBriefs_). Teammates are invited with the [team invitation](/emails/team-notifications/#team-invitation) email instead.

Alerts for a changed password, email address or phone number are turned off.
